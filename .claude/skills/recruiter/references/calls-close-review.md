# Call prep, closing & weekly review

## Workflow 6: Call & interview prep

Produce a one-page brief:
- **About them:** company, stack signals, likely pain (from the posting, site, LinkedIn).
- **His 60-second narrative:** Power BI reporting → full-stack Fabric engineering → what he wants to do for them.
- **Two STAR stories** with numbers (the 300-user org app rollout; the hours→minutes discovery system), plus one on CI/CD or migration if relevant.
- **Likely technical questions** for this role (e.g. Direct Lake fallback, RLS vs OLS, incremental refresh, deployment rules, dbt incremental strategies, capacity sizing) with crisp answers.
- **Questions to ask them** (3–5) that reveal budget, decision-maker and timeline.
- **Close:** the concrete next step to propose before the call ends (SOW tomorrow, paid assessment, technical screen date).

## Workflow 7: Close & deliver

- SOW within 24 h of a positive call: scope, deliverables, timeline, price, 50% upfront (or milestone escrow on a platform), change-request rate.
- Payments: Wise / Payoneer / Deel; check Turkey support for the client's country.
- First visible result within 3 days. At handover, ask for a testimonial, a LinkedIn recommendation and one introduction, then offer the retainer.

## Workflow 8: Weekly review

When he asks "how am I doing" or shares numbers: compute the funnel (messages → replies → calls → proposals → won), compare to benchmarks (cold email 3–6% reply, 1–2% call; warm/partner DMs 15–30% reply), name the weakest stage, and give the single change to make next week.
