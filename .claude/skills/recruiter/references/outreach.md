# Outreach, applications & recruiter communication

## Workflow 4: Outreach & applications

Choose the template by channel and adapt it. Every message carries: Fabric + DP-600, availability, remote, rate or "rate on request", and one proof point. Keep under 120 words unless it's a formal application.

- **Recruiter DM:** "Hi [Name], BI Engineer with Microsoft Fabric focus, DP-600 certified, available now for fully remote contract roles (1–3 months fine). Day rate [X]. Anything live on your Azure / Power BI desk? Happy to send a CV."
- **Partner practice lead:** a specific reference to their Fabric work + subcontract capacity from [date] + stack + 300-user proof + two options (day rate or fixed package) + a 15-min ask.
- **Cold email to a company (job-post trigger):** subject "Your [role] — a 4-week bridge while you hire"; the trigger; one-line proof + promo link; fixed offer with start date; 15-min ask; opt-out line.
- **Marketplace proposal:** line 1 restates their problem in their words; line 2 is DP-600 + the matching result; line 3 is price, start date and first deliverable in N days; then the call ask and portfolio link.
- **Forum → DM:** a real public answer first, then a DM with a walkthrough link and a soft offer.
- **Follow-up (once, day 5–7):** one line + new proof (latest video) + "if timing's off, who else owns Fabric on your side?" Then stop. Repeated chasing hurts.

Cadence for direct companies: email day 0 → LinkedIn connect day 3 → email day 7 → monthly "new video" nudge. Volume target: about 10 new personalised contacts a day. Never use LinkedIn automation tools, and stay under ~100 connection requests a week (accounts get restricted).

## Workflow 5: Recruiter & client communication

- Reply drafts: confirm interest, state availability, rate range, and remote/hours constraints up front, and ask the 4 questions that matter: client & project, contract length and IR35/contract type, rate and payment terms, interview steps.
- **Rate questions:** give a range anchored at or above his rate, then ask the budget. Never answer "flexible".
- **Right-to-represent:** before a recruiter submits him, ask for the client name and confirm the rate in writing. Don't let two agencies submit him to the same client.
- **Contract basics to check:** payment terms (net-15/30), currency, invoicing entity (Deel, Remote.com, direct B2B), IP clause, notice period, non-solicit scope.
