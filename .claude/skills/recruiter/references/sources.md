# Finding leads: source map & search tactics

Ask what he needs only if it isn't obvious. Otherwise pick the channels that fit and give concrete searches.

### Source map (verify availability before recommending)

**A. Direct-to-company (highest rate; the best consulting channel)**
- Job posts as triggers: LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed, Glassdoor, Wellfound, Welcome to the Jungle (ex-Otta), Himalayas, RemoteOK, We Work Remotely, Remotive, EU Remote Jobs. Search "Microsoft Fabric", "Power BI Developer", "BI Engineer", "Analytics Engineer", "dbt", posted in the last 7 days. A company hiring in-house has the problem now and faces 2–3 months of hiring, so pitch a **4-week bridge contract**.
- LinkedIn people search: title contains "Head of Data", "BI Manager", "Analytics Lead", "Data Platform", company size 51–500.
- Microsoft customer stories (Fabric filter), FabCon / SQLBits / Power BI user-group speakers and attendees, funding news (Crunchbase, weekly round-ups: Series A–B companies need a reporting layer).
- Email lookup: Apollo.io, Hunter.io. Keep cold email B2B, relevant, with an opt-out line.

**B. Microsoft partners (fastest cash: subcontracting)**
- Fabric Featured Partners directory (Microsoft Fabric partners page), mid-size firms and boutiques. Contact the Data & AI practice lead or delivery manager, not careers@.
- Known targets: Smartbridge, Collectiv, Simform, MDW, Acuvate; boutiques endjin, Data Bear, VNB Consulting, Transparity, P3 Adaptive, Coeo, Ventagium; SQLBI (training, well connected). Skip DMs to giants (Avanade, Hitachi, Accenture); they staff via agencies.

**C. Marketplaces**
- General: Upwork, Contra, Freelancer.com, Fiverr Pro (productized packages), PeoplePerHour (UK).
- Vetted networks: Toptal, Arc.dev, Proxify, Turing, Lemon.io, Index.dev, Braintrust, A.Team, Gun.io. Vetting takes 1–3 weeks and pay is often net-30, so they're month-two income.
- Regional: Malt (FR/DE/ES/BE/NL/CH/UK; usually needs a local business), freelancermap and Hays contracting (DACH), YunoJuno (UK).
- Tactics: bid only on posts < 24 h old; the first two lines restate the client's problem; fixed price + start date; accept one small fast job early for a review, then raise prices.

**D. Recruiters & staffing agencies (contract + perm)**
- Microsoft/data specialists: Nigel Frank (Tenth Revolution Group: apply via nigelfrank.com jobs and DM consultants; careers.tenthrevolution.com is their *internal* hiring), Harnham, Computer Futures (SThree), Hays Technology, Experis, TEKsystems, Randstad Digital, Robert Half.
- Big firms: EPAM Anywhere (country-restricted, 2–4 wk process, referral bonus motivates insiders), Accenture (slow, perm).
- Register with 3–4, not 10. Recruiters search by keywords and reward speed: reply within 2 hours in EU/UK working hours.

**E. Communities (warm leads, slow but compounding)**
- Fabric Community forum, r/MicrosoftFabric, r/PowerBI, r/dataengineering, dbt Slack (#jobs, #freelance), Power BI / Fabric user groups, LinkedIn comments on partner and MVP posts.
- Pattern: answer publicly and well, then DM with a matching video or offer.

**F. Content-led inbound**
- LinkedIn posts, YouTube tutorials (DP-600, Direct Lake, RLS, dbt + Fabric CI/CD), the 1-minute promo, vosk.dev. Every outreach message should link the most relevant proof piece.

### When asked "find me leads"
Return 5–15 specific leads or exact search strings, grouped by channel, each with: who/where, the trigger (why now), the angle (which package), and the first action. If web search is available, find real current postings and verify they're open.
