- **"does the current viz lib allow cross filtering: select a month on one chart and the whole dashboard filters?"** _(19:12)_ → **The library can do it, but it isn't wired up, and part of the data is too coarse for it.**
  - **The library isn't the limit.** The vendored Tremor `ComboChart` / `BarChart` expose `onValueChange`: a click on a bar or dot hands over its row (with `month`), a second click hands over `null`, and the other bars dim on their own. Tremor has no cross-filter engine. That's app state, and the dashboard already has it: one `SalesFilter`, from which every layer is re-derived in `useMemo`.
  - **Not wired today.** No chart gets `onValueChange`, and `SalesFilter` is `{ year }` only.
  - **The data grain is what limits it.** KPIs, the combo and the channel card come from `monthly` (month × channel), so a month click works on them client-side. The Top 5 is fed by `byCategory` / `byCountry` at **year** grain, so it can't follow a month until those two queries are `date_trunc('month', …)`. That is still 3 queries, with no Cube model change. The browser never talks to Cube, so filtering stays client-side on prefetched rows. Re-querying Cube per click is rejected (it would need a server hop, uses the free-tier quota, and adds latency). #idea
