## Watch / risks

- **DNS before compose up.** Caddy fetches its certificate on startup; a missing A record
  fails the whole stack in a way that reads as a Docker problem.
- **Verify on the deploy, not `localhost`.** Said on 07-21, 07-22, 07-23 — the email, showcase
  and accent debts all exist because this keeps being deferred. Superset adds a hard version
  of it: guest tokens live 300s, so an embed must be watched past six minutes or the refresh
  loop is untested.
- **Ops burden is now ours.** Self-hosting means Superset CVEs, deliberate image-tag bumps,
  and metadata backups. This was the real cost of `ADR-0003` and it starts the day the VPS
  goes up.
- **The 07-22 backlog is now nine days stale.** Two null weeks are how a "small backlog"
  becomes the reason a launch slips. Several of these are sub-hour items.
- Keep GTM/Resend/Superset env **optional** in validation — required vars break builds before
  keys exist.
