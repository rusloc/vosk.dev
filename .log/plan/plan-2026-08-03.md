# Plan — 2026-08-03

## Context

For the first time since 07-21 the board is not gated on "commit the batch" or "get a green deploy."
`main` builds, and everything through the SEO pass is pushed. That changes the top of the list: what
matters tomorrow is a **regression I shipped**, not a backlog.

The SEO pass on 08-02 was correct on signals and **wrong on restraint**. It fixed real defects — the
home page had no `<title>` at all, the Russian blog cluster was serving English prose under a `ru`
canonical, the fourth blog post existed nowhere a crawler reads — and then kept going into layout it
had no mandate to touch. Three of tomorrow's four new tasks are undoing that. That is the lesson of
the day and it is written up in `daily-2026-08-02`.

**None of the four new items is speculative — all of them are live on `vosk.dev` right now.**

## Priority 1 — Undo the front-page damage · **NEW, mine to fix, already deployed**

The instruction was "SEO the whole app." I read that as licence to add on-page content, and added
visible sections to a page that was deliberately a single hero. Keep the SEO, revert the layout.

### 1A. Restore the minimal front page

**What I added** ([`src/app/[locale]/page.tsx`](../../src/app/[locale]/page.tsx), commit `73eb4dc`):

- a `lede` paragraph under the `h1`;
- a **"What I do" section** — `h2` + a three-column `h3` grid with body copy and three links.

The second one is the junk. The page was a hero, a backdrop, a code-typer and one CTA; it is now a
marketing landing page. **Delete the `whatIDo` section entirely** (component markup + the `whatIDo`
block in `en.ts`/`ru.ts`), and keep a single short lede — see 1D for its copy.

**What must survive the revert, and where the SEO value actually lives:**

| Signal | Was it in the "What I do" section? | Where it lives without it |
|---|---|---|
| Query terms (Power BI, Superset, DAX, SQL, pipelines) | yes, and also | the lede, `meta.description`, and `knowsAbout[]` in `homeGraph()` |
| Internal links to `/work` `/services` `/blog` | yes | nav (all five), the two hero CTAs, footer |
| A second heading level | yes (`h2`+`h3`×3) | **lost — and that is fine.** A hero page with one `h1` is not a defect |

So the honest cost of the revert is close to zero **provided the lede stays**. A home page of ~8
words was genuinely thin; one or two real sentences fixes that without a section. Do not revert to
the pre-08-02 state — revert to hero + lede.

> **Decide before writing code:** is the `ctaSecondary` button ("See services & pricing", added the
> same day) also junk, or is a second hero CTA acceptable? It is one button on the existing row, not
> a new block. My read: keep it — it is the only crawlable path from `/` to the money page. **User's
> call.**

### 1B. Remove the menu duplication on the start page

I added a footer nav block ([`src/components/footer.tsx`](../../src/components/footer.tsx)) listing
all five nav items. On a five-page site it restates the header verbatim, and on the hero-only home
page the duplication is the most visible thing on screen.

**Remove the footer nav block.** The rationale for adding it — "important pages one hop from a single
source" — does not hold at this size: the header nav already links every page from every page, and a
second identical set adds no crawl path Google didn't have. Keep the footer as it was: copyright,
lang switcher, RSS, availability.

### 1C. Remove tags from the blog post page

Per-post tag chips render via `PostMetaRow` at
[`blog/[slug]/page.tsx`](../../src/app/[locale]/blog/[slug]/page.tsx).

⚠️ **Scope this to the post page only.** `PostMetaRow` is shared — `blog-list.tsx` renders the same
component for the list cards, and the tag chips there are wanted. Add a `showTags` prop (default
`true`) and pass `false` from the post page; do **not** delete the tags branch from the component.

✅ **Verified safe for SEO:** `/blog` carries an always-visible tag row (`TagLink`, `blog/page.tsx:57`)
added on 08-01 *specifically* so the tag pages were reachable by mobile crawlers. Every tag page
keeps an entry point after this change. Re-grep after editing to confirm — this is the exact class
of thing that quietly orphans 10 pages.

### 1D. Rephrase the hero sub-title

Current lede (`en.ts` `home.lede`, written 08-02):

> "I'm a freelance Business Intelligence analyst and developer. I build Power BI and Apache Superset
> dashboards, the data models and SQL pipelines underneath them, and the automation that keeps the
> numbers arriving without anyone asking."

It is three clauses long, reads like a CV summary, and repeats the `<h1>`'s idea instead of extending
it. Rewrite: **one or two sentences**, plain, extending "Save money by understanding your data"
rather than restating it. Must still carry the primary terms (see 1A table) since it becomes the only
body copy on the page. **Draft 2–3 options for the user to pick, both locales** — do not pick
silently, this is the first sentence a client reads.

### Verify 1A–1D together

`pnpm typecheck && pnpm lint && pnpm build`, then **look at the page in a browser** — this is a
visual regression, and the whole reason it happened is that I verified 08-02's work by grepping HTML
and never opened it. Then re-check: `/` still has a `<title>`, still has `og:image`, `homeGraph()`
still emits, `/blog/tag/*` still linked from `/blog`.

## Priority 2 — The highest-value unblocked code bug (carried, **verified still open**)

**An empty-string `RESEND_API_KEY` 500s the contact form.** `getContactConfig()` is called *outside*
the try/catch in `src/app/[locale]/contact/actions.ts` (confirmed 08-02: the call sits ~12 lines
above the `try`). `""` is present-but-invalid under `z.string().min(1)`, so a ZodError escapes the
Server Action.

The bitter part is unchanged: `contact-config.ts` exists precisely so a missing key degrades to
"email me directly" — and that fallback is unreachable in exactly the operator mistake it was built
for. **Carried since 07-31. Still the highest-value item nobody is blocking.** It breaks the site's
only conversion path.

   locale dimension at all. **Decide the shape before any Russian prose is written.** Unblocking this
   is what lets the `noindex` come off.
3. **`three-numbers` 404** — decide redirect vs leave.
4. **iframe `sandbox` hardening** on `/work/[slug]`.
5. **Turnstile** — adopt or explicitly drop; honeypot + rate limit are in place and tested.
6. **Rate-limit durability** — in-memory `Map` in two places, grows unbounded (one entry per IP,
   forever). Harmless on serverless; extract to `lib/rate-limit.ts` if a third caller appears.
7. **Test coverage gaps** — `escapeHtml` (`lib/contact-email.ts`) is the only guard between visitor
   input and email HTML and is untested; `content.ts`'s "fail the build loudly" guarantee is

9. **Audit what else is "believed shipped but isn't."** Commit `374db5a`'s message claimed tag pages,

## Watch / risks

- **I verified 08-02's work without ever looking at it.** Every gate was green, every curl assertion
  passed, and the front page still came out wrong — because "does the HTML contain the right tags"
  and "does the page look right" are different questions and I only asked the first. **The 07-31→08-01
  refrain was "build the artifact, then look at the artifact." I built it and grepped it.** Open the
  browser tomorrow, on the deploy.
- **Scope discipline.** "SEO the whole app" is not licence to redesign the home page. When an SEO fix
  requires a *visual* change, that is a proposal, not an implementation.
- **The regression is already public** — pushed 08-02, so it is on `vosk.dev` now. Not an emergency
  (it is ugly, not broken) but it is the live first impression on a site whose entire job is credibility.
- **Do not let the `noindex`es become permanent.** Two were shipped 08-02 as *containment* — the RU
  blog cluster and `retail-sales` — and both have a named unblocking condition. They are a holding
  pattern, not a decision. Re-read this line in a month.
- **DNS before `compose up`.** Caddy fetches its certificate on startup.
- **Ops burden starts the day the VPS goes up** — Superset CVEs, image-tag bumps, metadata backups.
- Keep GTM / Resend / Superset env **optional** in validation.
