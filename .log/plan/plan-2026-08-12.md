carried on the file's word:

- `contact-config.ts:16` types the key `z.string().min(1).optional()`, so `""` is

- `getContactConfig()` is called at `contact/actions.ts:67`, ten lines above the `try` at `:77`.

  (`actions.ts:68-73`) — and that fallback is unreachable in exactly the operator mistake it was

degradation honest at the source instead of catching a throw. **Land the unit test in the same
commit**; the harness exists.
