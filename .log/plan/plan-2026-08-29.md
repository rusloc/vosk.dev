`enrich`'s `persist()` hard-requires `raw_payload._company_id` and reads `payload.title`. That
contract exists **only because Himalayas happens to publish `companySlug`.**

lack a company field.** Everything that has one sits at 1–3% and skews large-employer FTE.

~20 lines: add `company_name` to the `record_lead` tool schema; in `persist()`, look up or create the
company when the collector resolved none. **Backward-compatible — Himalayas still passes
`_company_id` and is untouched.** Deferred on 08-28 priced as a one-source cost for HN; the sweep
showed it gates both.

21, but `Data analyst | Chicago, IL` leads with the role and `Snout  https://snout.com/` glues the URL
into field 0. A splitter writes junk `companies` rows **instead of erroring**.
