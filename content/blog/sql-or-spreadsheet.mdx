Spreadsheets are the fastest way to answer a question _once_. The trouble starts
the second time you need the same answer — now the logic lives in cell formulas
only one person understands.

My rule of thumb:

- **One-off, throwaway, exploratory?** Spreadsheet. Ship the answer, move on.
- **Repeated, shared, or feeding a decision?** SQL. Version it, name the columns,
  make it auditable.

The moment a number has to be _reproducible_, move it out of the sheet. Future
you — and everyone who trusts that number — will thank you.
