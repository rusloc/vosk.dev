Most dashboard rebuilds trace back to a definition nobody pinned down. Before I
open a query editor, I get three numbers agreed on in writing:

- **What counts as one _active_ user?** Logged in? Took an action? Over what window?
- **What is "revenue" here?** Gross, net of refunds, recognised, or booked?
- **What's the reporting grain?** Daily, weekly, per-account — pick one and stick to it.

Five minutes of agreement here saves days of "the number looks wrong" later. A
dashboard is only as trustworthy as the definitions underneath it.
